Difaac team portraits
======================

Drop the three founders' photos here, named exactly:

  farah.jpg
  sharmaarke.jpg
  khaalid.jpg

They appear automatically on the Team page ("The Leadership") and on the
About page's founders grid. A portrait shows a monogram until its file is
added, so the pages never show a broken image.

Recommended: a portrait-orientation photo, at least 600 x 750 px, roughly a
4:5 ratio (the grid crops to 4:5). JPG or PNG.
